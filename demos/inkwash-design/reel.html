一笔一画Stroke by stroke

同一支笔依次画出:墨分五色、同一座山的四种设色、一张技术框图、刘徽割圆术、《江雪》、一个书法数字。所有画面走同一个时钟,拖进度条看任意一刻;scripts/export.mjs 用同一个时钟逐帧截图编成视频。One brush paints, in turn: five ink tones, one mountain in four colourings, an architecture diagram, Liu Hui’s circle, "River Snow" and a brush numeral. Everything runs on one clock; scrub to any moment. scripts/export.mjs uses the same clock to export a video frame by frame.
