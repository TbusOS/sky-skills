定死的只有五样Five things are fixed

这五样就是这个画风,别的都随你These five are the look. Everything else is yours.

风格不靠模板,靠材料。换掉其中一样,画面就不是这个画风了;这五样都在,版式、构图、角色、动画怎么做都还是它。The look comes from materials, not a template. Drop one of these and it stops being this style; keep all five and any layout, composition, cast or motion still reads as it.

1 · 暖纸1 · Warm paper

米色纸底,不用纯白。深色版是藏青墨纸加粉笔线,另画一张,不能把浅色图反相。Cream paper, never pure white. Dark mode is navy paper with chalk lines, drawn again, never an inverted copy.

2 · 石墨线2 · Graphite line

深灰褐,不是纯黑。线会轻微抖,转角处冲出去一点,画的圈首尾对不齐。Warm dark grey, never black. It wobbles, runs a little past corners, and loops do not quite close.

3 · 少量彩铅3 · A few pencils

颜色是来回涂的彩铅线,不是平涂。一张图里一种颜色只干一件事,大片纸面空着。Colour is back-and-forth pencil, not flat fill. Each colour has one job in a figure, and most of the paper stays empty.

手写的字handwritten

4 · 手写批注4 · Hand lettering

图里的说明用手写体(文楷);代码和数字用等宽字。插画本身不写字,字由网页叠上去。Labels in a hand font (WenKai); code and numbers monospaced. Illustrations carry no words; the page lays text on top.

5 · 先线后色,一笔一笔5 · Ink, then colour

像一个人在画:按故事顺序,先勾线再上色(时间大约 2:1)。关掉动画时直接显示画好的样子。Like a person drawing: in story order, outline first and colour after (about 2:1). With motion off, the finished drawing shows.

动起来Motion

拖一下进度条:每一秒的画面都是定的Drag the slider: every second is a fixed picture

动画全是 CSS,时间由代码算好写进去。所以能暂停、能逐帧拖,导出视频时第 300 帧永远是第 10 秒那个样子。The motion is plain CSS with times computed by code. So it pauses, scrubs frame by frame, and frame 300 of an exported video is always what second 10 looks like.

0.0 s
  1. 一支笔。One pen. 同一时间只画一样东西,每一笔的时长和它的长度成正比。One thing at a time; each stroke takes time in proportion to its length.
  2. 按故事顺序。Story order. 先场景,再主角,再动作,最后结果。不是从左到右扫。Setting, then the subject, then the action, then the result. Not a left-to-right sweep.
  3. 先线后色。Ink, then colour. 每组里勾线占三分之二的时间,上色占三分之一。In each group, outlines take two thirds of the time and colour one third.
  4. 一个时钟。One clock. 画面、代码高亮、计数都挂在同一条时间线上,所以永远对得上。Picture, code highlight and counter hang on one timeline, so they always agree.
  5. 关掉动画也完整。Complete with motion off. 系统设置了「减少动态效果」,就直接显示画好的结果。With reduced motion set, the finished drawing shows at once.
越用越懂你It learns your taste

挑你喜欢的那一张,它会记住Pick the one you like. It remembers.

每一题只改一个参数,左右两张只差这一处。你选一次,参数就往你选的那边挪一点;说一句「颜色太多了」也算。审查器查出的客观问题(比如手机上字太小)会定一条下限,口味再怎么变也不越过去。下面的结果只存在你自己的浏览器里。Each question changes one setting; the two drawings differ only there. Every pick moves that setting a little your way; saying "too many colours" counts too. Objective problems a reviewer finds (labels too small on a phone) set a floor that taste never crosses. What you do here stays in your own browser.
