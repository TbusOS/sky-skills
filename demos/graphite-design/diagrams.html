27 张手绘图例27 hand-drawn figures

22 张会动,滚到哪张就画哪张;其余 5 张按「看它怎么画」也能一笔一笔重画。每张都是几十行代码,拿去改比照着画快:换字、换颜色、换数据。手机上图框可以左右拖,图里的字不会缩到看不清。22 are animated and draw when you scroll to them; the other 5 redraw stroke by stroke on "Watch it drawn". Each is a few dozen lines of code: changing words, colours or data beats redrawing. On a phone, drag a figure sideways; its labels never shrink below readable.

角色与道具Cast and props

能反复出场的小角色和常用物件。拿去换姿势、换颜色,拼成自己的场景。Small characters and everyday objects to reuse. Change the pose or colour and build your own scene.

场景插画Scenes

一幅画讲一个比喻:谁在做什么。画面里不写字,字放在网页上叠上去。One picture, one metaphor: who is doing what. No words inside the drawing; the page lays text on top.

电影画风Cinematic

宽银幕、一个光源、镜头在动。同一支铅笔,换成讲故事的节奏。Widescreen, one light source, a moving camera. The same pencil, paced like a film.

框图与架构Block and architecture

方框、分层、连线。手绘让结构图不像规范文档,读者更愿意看下去。Boxes, layers and wires. Drawn by hand, a structure diagram stops looking like a spec.

流程图Flowcharts

判断、分支、回路。会动的那一笔沿着流程走,告诉读者现在走到哪。Decisions, branches and loops. The moving mark walks the flow and shows where we are.

代码大白话Code in plain words

左边是代码,右边是它在做的事。当前那一行和画面同步高亮。Code on one side, what it does on the other. The current line and the picture move together.

数学与算法Maths and algorithms

把公式和算法的每一步画出来:球往山下滚、数组一半一半地砍。Each step of a formula or algorithm, drawn: a ball rolling downhill, an array cut in half.

数据图Charts

手绘坐标轴,真实数据。点和线是铅笔画的,数字照样要对。Hand-drawn axes, real numbers. The dots are pencil; the values still have to be right.

标注与叠层Marks and overlays

圈重点、画箭头、贴便签、深色终端卡:叠在插画或截图上用。Circles, arrows, sticky notes and a dark terminal card, laid over a drawing or a screenshot.
