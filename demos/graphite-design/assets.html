竖屏视频封面Vertical video covers

图里一个字都没有,上面留一大块空白;标题是网页上的字,叠在图上,改字不用重新出图。发到短视频平台时,右边一列和底部会被按钮、文案挡住,标题放在上面这块空白里正好避开(见图例库「短视频画面」)。No words in the pictures, and a large empty band at the top. The title is page text laid on top, so changing it needs no new image. On short-video apps the right column and the bottom are covered by buttons and captions; a title in the top band stays clear of both (see "Video frame" in the figures).

角色姿势表Character pose sheets

同一套角色,和首屏插画是同一个机器人、同一个人。背景是纸色(约 #fcf7e5),不是透明的:放在浅色页面上加 mix-blend-mode: multiply,纸色就和页面融在一起;要单个姿势,裁下来用。The same cast as the hero picture: same robot, same person. The background is paper (about #fcf7e5), not transparent: on a light page add mix-blend-mode: multiply and the paper melts into the page; crop out any single pose you need.

代码画的 SVG:贴纸、批注、视频画面Code-drawn SVG: stickers, marks, video frames

单独的 SVG 文件跟随系统深浅色,打开就会自己画一遍。要 PNG 或 MP4:node skills/graphite-design/scripts/export.mjs png 文件.svg 输出.png --scale=3。The standalone SVGs follow the system light/dark setting and draw themselves when opened. For PNG or MP4: node skills/graphite-design/scripts/export.mjs png file.svg out.png --scale=3.
