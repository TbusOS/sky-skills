About the numbers on these figures 关于图上那些数字

The structure is checked; the numbers are illustrative. Which block issues a read, and which driver owns the DMA, was read out of mainline Linux — every such claim on this page names its file and line, and the tree is v7.0-9887-ga43fac2b5813. The bus widths, clock rates and capacities are one representative configuration, chosen so the orders of magnitude line up; they are not the datasheet of any particular part, and nothing here was taken from a vendor manual. The argument rests only on the ratio — a frame is megabytes, on-chip memory is kilobytes — and that gap is three orders of magnitude on any chip of this class. 结构是查过的，数字是示意的。哪个模块发读请求、DMA 归哪个驱动管，是从主线 Linux 源码里读出来的——本页每条这类论断都给了文件名和行号，树是 v7.0-9887-ga43fac2b5813。总线位宽、频率、容量是一种代表性配置，为的是让量级对得上；它们不是某颗具体芯片的规格书，也没有任何一个数是从厂商手册抄的。论点只依赖那个比例——一帖是兆字节，片上存储是千字节——这个差距在任何这类芯片上都有三个数量级。

The premise前提

A framebuffer is an address, not a package. 帖缓冲是一个地址，不是一个包裹。

Physically, showing an image is not a delivery. It is one hardware block reading the same region of DDR over and over on a fixed beat, and shipping what it reads out over a serial link. Software’s entire contribution is a single 32-bit register write saying where that region begins. Every read after that is issued by the display controller’s own DMA engine, without asking anyone.

从物理上讲，“显示一张图”不是一次投递。它是一个硬件模块按固定节拍反复去读 DDR 上同一块区域，把读到的字节从串行链路发出去。软件的全部工作，是写一个 32 位寄存器告诉硬件那块区域从哪里开始。之后每一次读取，都由显示控制器自己的 DMA 发起，不再经过任何人。

Take that DMA engine away and the CPU has to move the pixels itself. At 1080p60 that is 8.3 MB sixty times a second — on the order of 62 million 64-bit load/store pairs per second, which saturates a core outright. Worse, any interrupt that delays the copy lands on the glass as a visible tear. So the DMA is not an optimisation layered on a working design; it is what makes “the screen refreshes while the CPU does something else” possible at all.

把这个 DMA 拿掉，像素就得 CPU 自己搬。1080p60 是每秒 8.3 MB × 60，数量级上是每秒 6200 万次 64 位读写配对，一个核直接被占满。更糟的是，任何一次中断让拷贝晚了，屏幕上就是一条看得见的撕裂。所以这个 DMA 不是在一个能用的设计上再做优化，它是“屏幕在刷新的同时 CPU 还能干别的”这件事能成立的前提。

Setting a framebuffer is a register write, not a copy. Four bytes go one way; 8.3 megabytes a frame come the other — and they never travel on the same wire. 设置帖缓冲是一次寄存器写，不是一次拷贝。四个字节往一个方向去，每帖 8.3 MB 从另一个方向来——两者从不走同一根线。

The trade-off取舒

Three designs were on the table. Two of them cannot be built. 有三种设计可选。其中两种造不出来。

Alternative A方案 A

Keep the frame on-chip把帖放在片内

One frame is 8.3 MB. The largest on-die memory in the second figure is the GPU’s L2 cache at 256 KiB — short by a factor of 32. On-chip SRAM also costs tens of times more silicon area per bit than DRAM, so even a die that could hold a frame would not be one anybody buys. 一帖是 8.3 MB。第二张图里片上最大的一块存储是 GPU 的 L2，256 KiB，差 32 倍。片内 SRAM 每比特的硅片面积还是 DRAM 的几十倍，就算做得出来也没人买得起。

verdict: not buildable结论：造不出来

Alternative B方案 B

One memory per master每个主设备各一块内存

Give the GPU and the display controller separate memories and a frame has to be genuinely copied between them: 8.3 MB sixty times a second is 498 MB/s of pure moving, repeated every time the compositor switches buffers. Sharing one DDR instead makes that number zero — the cost drops to passing an address. 给 GPU 和显示控制器各一块内存，一帖就得在两者之间真的拷一遍：8.3 MB × 60，每秒 498 MB 的纯搬运，而且合成器每换一次缓冲就再来一遍。改成共用一块 DDR，这个数字是 0——代价限于传一个地址。

verdict: buildable, pays 498 MB/s for nothing结论：造得出，但白付 498 MB/s

What shipped实际采用的

One DDR, many masters一块 DDR，多个主设备

Every block issues its own addresses into the same physical memory. The price is that bandwidth becomes a shared resource: five tenants against 6.4 GB/s in the second figure, more once a camera and a codec join. That is why the DDR controller carries QoS classes at all — the display port is served first, because a late GPU costs one dropped frame while late scanout is visible on the glass. 每个模块自己向同一块物理内存发地址。代价是带宽成了公共资源：第二张图里五个租户分 6.4 GB/s，加上摄像头和编解码还会更多。DDR 控制器那套优先级就是为此存在——显示端口先服务，因为 GPU 晚了只是掉一帖，扫描输出晚了是看得见的。

verdict: shipped · bandwidth becomes the shared resource结论：采用 · 带宽成为共享资源

Putting the finished frame on screen costs four bytes 把画好的一帖放到屏上，代价是四个字节

This is the step the first two figures leave out, and the one where people most often assume a copy happens. It does not. The two buffers stay exactly where they are; a register learns a different number. 这是前两张图没讲的一步，也是最多人以为发生了拷贝的一步。没有。两块缓冲原地不动，变的是一个寄存器里的数。

Figure 04 · discrete card图 04 · 独立显卡

Where there really are two memories, and the copy is real 真的有两块内存的那种机器，拷贝是真的

The picture starts in ordinary system memory either way. On a desktop card it then has to cross to a second address space, and this figure draws that crossing — because it is the step everybody means by upload, and it is the one the first two figures do not have. 不管哪种机器，图一开始都在普通系统内存里。到了台式机的显卡上，它得跨到第二个地址空间去——这张图画的就是这一跨。大家说上传时指的正是它，而前两张图里根本没有这一步。

So which one are you on那你手上是哪一种

The test is whether a second memory exists at all判断依据只有一条：有没有第二块内存

A phone-class chip and an integrated desktop GPU both share one DDR with the CPU, so there is no second address space to cross and no copy to optimise. A discrete card has its own VRAM behind a link, so the copy is real, expensive, and worth doing once and keeping. Both cases are in these three figures; the vocabulary that confuses people came from the second and got applied to the first. 手机芯片和桌面机的集成显卡，都是跟 CPU 共用一块 DDR，没有第二个地址空间要跨，也就没有拷贝可优化。独立显卡隔着一根链路有自己的显存，拷贝是真的、是贵的，值得拷一次然后一直放那儿。这三张图把两种都画了；让人淩涤的那套词汇来自第二种，却被用到了第一种身上。

When it matters什么时候用得上

Four moments where the wrong mental model costs you a day 四个时刻：脑子里的图错了，就是一天

  • The screen tears or flickers and you have to decide whether the fetch fell behind or the panel timing is wrong. Look at DDR bandwidth contention first — the DSI registers are further down the path than the problem usually is. 屏幕撕裂或闪一下，要判断是取数没跟上还是面板时序不对。先看 DDR 带宽争用——DSI 寄存器在通路更靠后的位置，比问题实际在的地方更远。
  • Display starts stuttering only after a camera preview is added. A sixth tenant just joined the same 6.4 GB/s; nothing about the display path changed. 加了一路摄像头预览之后显示开始抵。是第六个租户挤进了同一条 6.4 GB/s，显示通路本身一点没改。
  • Somebody proposes “upload the image to the GPU and then display it.” On this class of chip there is no upload — only a write to a different address. 有人说“先把图上传到 GPU 再显示”。在这类芯片上没有上传，只有写到另一个地址。
  • You are hunting for the DMA registers. They are in the display controller driver. The DSI bridge does not read memory at all — pixels are handed to it. 你在找 DMA 相关的寄存器。它们在显示控制器驱动里。DSI 桥根本不读内存——像素是别人嗞给它的。
The mechanism机制细节

The wires, and the lines of source that pin each claim down 那几根线，以及截住每条论断的源码行

Config path配置路

APB · 32-bit Software writes the framebuffer base address register once per frame — four bytes. A pixel never travels on this wire. APB · 32-bit 软件每帖写一次帖缓冲基地址寄存器，四个字节。这根线上永远不会走像素。

Data path数据路

AXI · 128-bit The display controller’s fetch DMA issues reads on the scanout beat — 8.3 MB a frame. Its line buffer holds a few lines, never a whole frame. AXI · 128-bit 显示控制器的 fetch DMA 按扫描节拍发读请求，每帖 8.3 MB。它的行缓冲只存几行，从来不存整帖。

Pixel interface像素接口

24-bit RGB → D-PHY From display controller to DSI controller is a parallel pixel bus, not AXI. The DSI block packs it into high-speed packets and the D-PHY drives four lanes off-chip. 24-bit RGB → D-PHY 从显示控制器到 DSI 控制器这一段是并行像素总线，不是 AXI。DSI 把它打成高速包，D-PHY 推四条 lane 发出芯片。

Evidence · on a SoC实证 · SoC 那两张

In drivers/gpu/drm/bridge/synopsys/dw-mipi-dsi.c, grep -c dma_addr returns 0 — the DSI bridge holds no buffer and issues no reads. The write that arms the hardware is vop2_win_write(win, VOP2_WIN_YRGB_MST, yrgb_mst) at drivers/gpu/drm/rockchip/rockchip_drm_vop2.c:1370. That the GPU has its own MMU, and that its page tables sit in ordinary memory, is in drivers/gpu/drm/panfrost/panfrost_regs.h and panfrost_mmu.c:820. 在 drivers/gpu/drm/bridge/synopsys/dw-mipi-dsi.c 里跑 grep -c dma_addr 得到 0——DSI 桥不持有缓冲，也不发读请求。真正把硬件装好的那一行是 drivers/gpu/drm/rockchip/rockchip_drm_vop2.c:1370 的 vop2_win_write(win, VOP2_WIN_YRGB_MST, yrgb_mst)。GPU 有自己的 MMU、且页表住在普通内存里，见 drivers/gpu/drm/panfrost/panfrost_regs.h 与 panfrost_mmu.c:820。

Evidence · discrete实证 · 独立显卡那张

include/drm/ttm/ttm_placement.h:51 names the three placements — TTM_PL_SYSTEM, TTM_PL_TT, TTM_PL_VRAM. include/uapi/drm/amdgpu_drm.h:89 documents GTT as “GPU accessible system memory, mapped into the GPU’s virtual address space via gart”. The copy runs amdgpu_move_blit → amdgpu_copy_buffer (amdgpu_ttm.c:383, :2484) on mman.buffer_funcs_ring, which sdma_v5_2.c:2060 binds to the SDMA ring. Scanout is forced into VRAM at amdgpu_display.c:560. include/drm/ttm/ttm_placement.h:51 定义了三种归属——TTM_PL_SYSTEM、TTM_PL_TT、TTM_PL_VRAM。include/uapi/drm/amdgpu_drm.h:89 把 GTT 解释为“GPU 能访问的系统内存，经由 gart 映射进 GPU 的虚拟地址空间”。拷贝走的是 amdgpu_move_blit → amdgpu_copy_buffer（amdgpu_ttm.c:383、:2484），用 mman.buffer_funcs_ring，而 sdma_v5_2.c:2060 把那个 ring 绑到 SDMA。扫描输出被强制放在 VRAM，见 amdgpu_display.c:560。
