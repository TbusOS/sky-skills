found in 25 flips 2000 万张卡片，
25 次就能翻到

No maths background needed. One card a second, twenty million takes over seven months; halving takes 25. 不需要数学基础。一秒一张地翻，2000 万张要看七个多月；对半砍只要 25 次。

What is halving like? 对半砍像什么？

二分查找 binary searchbinary search turn over only the middle one, then drop a whole half 每次只翻中间那一张，然后扔掉一半

In a guessing game you don't count up from one. You say the middle number — a wrong guess still drops half. 猜数字游戏里，你不会从 1 开始一个个报。你每次报中间那个数——报错了也砍掉一半。

Guess a number from 1 to 100: start at 50. Too high, try 25; too low, try 75 — seven guesses always get there. 猜 1 到 100 的数：先猜 50。高了猜 25，低了猜 75——七次之内一定中。

Why is 25 enough? 为什么 25 次就够？

Halving is doubling run backwards. Start at one card, double 25 times, and you reach 33,554,432 — past twenty million. 对半砍的反面是翻倍。从 1 张开始翻 25 次，到 33 554 432 张——比 2000 万多。

Why must it already be in order? 为什么必须先排好序？

索引 indexindex a copy something keeps in order for you, all the time 有人一直替你保持着顺序的那份副本

Out of order, the middle card tells you nothing. 没排好序，翻中间那一张什么也说明不了。

Now you know 现在你知道了

  • Binary search turns over the middle card and drops the half it cannot be in. 二分查找就是翻开中间那一张，然后扔掉不可能的那一半。
  • Twenty million cards take 25 cuts; one at a time takes over seven months. 2000 万张卡片 25 刀就到，一张一张翻要七个多月。
  • It only works if the cards are already in order by the number you search on. 前提是这排卡片一直按你要查的号码排好序。
