Six things you
cannot see,
one page each 看不见的那几样，
一页画一样

From one row of switches to a whole chip, and on to how a keystroke reaches a wire. No hardware background needed on any of the six — about four minutes a page. 从一排开关，到一颗芯片，再到你敲的一个字怎么走到线上。六页都不需要硬件基础，一页大约 4 分钟。

What is this set? 这六页是什么？

数据手册datasheet the manual a chip maker ships with the part — these six pages have to match it 芯片厂随芯片一起给的说明书，这六页画的东西都要在它上面对得上

All six hide inside the chip; the lid shows nothing. Each page opens with an analogy, then draws by the datasheet's rules. 这六样都藏在芯片里，壳上什么也看不见。每页先给一个日常比喻，再按手册的规矩画。

It's like…就像……

A hospital film. What you cannot see from outside is right there on the sheet, close enough to point at. 医院拍的片子。从外面看不见的东西，在片子上摆着，可以指着一样一样讲。

Six pages, one question each 六页，各答一个问题

The first four stay inside the chip; the fifth walks from a keystroke to a wire. The sixth is about finding things fast — no fixed order, start where you like. 前四页在芯片里面，第五页从你敲的键走到线上。第六页讲一个找东西的办法，顺序不锁，挑你现在想懂的那一页。

Now you know 现在你知道了

  • These six pages open up what the chip keeps sealed: switches, beats, streets, blocks. 这六页把封在芯片里的东西画开：开关、拍子、街道、积木。
  • Each page answers one question — analogy first, then the real drawing. 每页只答一个问题，先给日常比喻，再画真的那一样。
  • Every number and every order drawn here matches the maker's own manual. 图上的每个数字、每一步次序，都对得上芯片厂的手册。

The drawing language itself lives in the demo and the picture gallery. 这套画法本身在 demo 和 图解集 里。
