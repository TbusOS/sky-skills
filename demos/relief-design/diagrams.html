HOW TO PICK ONE怎么挑

Ask what the reader will do with the answer. A figure that shows containment answers "what goes away with what"; one that shows order answers "what happens next"; one that shows an interval answers "when is it unsafe". Most wrong diagrams are the right picture of the wrong one of those three.先问读者拿到答案要干什么。画包含的图回答「关掉这个会连带关掉谁」；画顺序的回答「下一步发生什么」；画区间的回答「什么时候是不安全的」。大多数画错的图，是把这三样里选错的那一样画对了。
