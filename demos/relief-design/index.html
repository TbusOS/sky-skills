2 · Where it is allowed to sleep2 · 哪一段允许睡眠

The chip needs an i2c read to hand over the coordinates, and i2c sleeps. The interrupt handler cannot. So the work is split across two contexts, and the band a function sits in decides what it may call — the ringed band is the one where a single i2c_transfer() would deadlock the machine.要拿到坐标得走一次 i2c 读，而 i2c 会睡眠，中断处理函数不能睡。所以活被劈成两段，一个函数在哪条带里，决定了它能调什么 —— 描圈那条带里，一次 i2c_transfer() 就能把机器锁死。

3 · What the chip actually hands over3 · 芯片到底交出来什么

The first register read is 16 bits and it decides everything that follows. Reserved bits are drawn sunken, so you know which ones you may not write without reading the access column. The write-1-to-clear bit carries the accent because it is the one people get wrong — it always reads back 0, and that does not mean your write missed.第一次寄存器读是 16 位，后面所有事都由它决定。保留位画成凹陷，不用读属性列就知道哪些不能写。写 1 清除的那位给强调色，因为它是最容易出错的一个 —— 它读回来永远是 0，而这不代表你那次写没生效。
