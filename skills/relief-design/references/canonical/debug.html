Six figures you open while the bug is still on screen六张 bug 还挂在屏幕上时会打开的图

Not diagrams for learning how something works — diagrams for holding next to a failure. Each one answers a question that comes up while you are already stuck: why nothing printed, who is waiting on whom, what the count was when it died, and which interval the bug actually lives in.不是用来搞懂原理的图 —— 是出事的时候摆在旁边看的图。每一张回答一个「你已经卡住了才会问」的问题：为什么什么都没打印、谁在等谁、死的时候计数是多少、bug 到底活在哪一段区间里。

1 · Why probe never ran1 · probe 为什么没跑

Five gates, and the first three fail silently — no error, no log line, nothing. That is what makes this the question every driver author asks first. The accent marks the first gate that is inside your own code: if the dev_info() at the top of probe does not print, the problem is above that line, and reading your driver harder will not find it.五道关卡，前三道挡住你的时候一声不吭 —— 不报错，不打日志，什么都没有。这就是为什么它是每个写驱动的人第一个要问的问题。强调色标的是第一道进到你自己代码里的关卡：probe 开头那句 dev_info() 没打印，问题就在这条线以上，再怎么细读自己的驱动也找不着。

2 · Who holds what2 · 锁的持有关系

A lock you hold is a thing you have, so it is drawn raised. A lock you are waiting for is an empty socket, so it is drawn sunken and ringed. 3 · Reference count lifetime3 · 引用计数生命周期

Each row is one get or put and what the count became. While the object is alive the row is raised; once the count hits zero the object is freed and the last row collapses into a groove. A call that lands on a sunken row is reaching into memory that is gone — that is the whole shape of a use-after-free.每一行是一次 get 或 put，以及计数变成了多少。对象活着的时候行是凸起的；计数一归零对象被释放，最后那行塌成一道凹槽。落在凹行上的调用，就是往已经没有了的内存里伸手 —— 释放后使用的全部形状就是这样。

4 · The race window4 · 竞态窗口

Two lanes share one time axis, and between them sits a sunken band — the window. Everything outside it is fine; the bug exists only while both sides have read the old value and neither has written back. Drawing the window as a groove says that without a sentence: the danger is not an instruction, it is an interval.两条泳道共用一根时间轴，中间夹着一条凹带 —— 那就是窗口。窗口外面一切正常；bug 只存在于两边都读到了旧值、而谁都还没写回去的这段时间里。把窗口画成凹槽，不用写一句话就说清了：危险的不是某条指令，是一段区间。

5 · Reading an oops5 · oops 怎么读

The raw dump goes in a sunken panel, because that is what it is: a screen. Beside each line is what it actually tells you. Most of the value in an oops is in the three lines people skip — the fault address is usually not 0, and the offset is the struct field.原始 dump 放进凹陷面板，因为它本来就是一块屏。每一行旁边写它到底在说什么。一份 oops 的价值大半在被跳过的那三行里 —— 出错地址通常不是 0，那个偏移就是结构体里的字段。

6 · Why it never woke up6 · 它为什么没醒

Same two lanes, same shape, different question. The sleeper is sunken because it is in the queue; the waker is raised because it is running.
