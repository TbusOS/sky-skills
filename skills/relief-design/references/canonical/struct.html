1 · What a struct actually occupies1 · 结构体到底占了多少

The fields you wrote are on the left, what the compiler laid out is on the right — and the padding is drawn as sunken gaps, because that is what it is: nothing. Two holes here cost 15 of 56 bytes. The dashed line is the cache line boundary, which is the reason this matters beyond tidiness.你写的字段在左边，编译器排出来的在右边 —— 而填充画成凹陷的空格，因为它就是这个：什么都没有。这里两个空洞吃掉了 56 字节里的 15 个。那条虚线是 cache line 边界，也是这件事不只是「整不整齐」的原因。

2 · Three relations that look alike in code2 · 代码里长得像的三种关系

struct B b, struct B *b and struct list_head n are one character apart in the source and three different lifetimes in reality. Embedding is drawn as containment, a pointer as a hop out to somewhere else, and a list_head as neither — because the list holds the head, not your struct.struct B b、struct B *b、struct list_head n 在源码里差一个字符，在现实里是三条不同的生命周期。嵌入画成包含，指针画成跳到别处，而 list_head 两者都不是 —— 因为链表串的是那个 head，不是你的结构体。

3 · Where your private pointer is parked3 · 你的私有指针存在哪

Four lines of probe, drawn. The kernel hands you a struct device; you allocate your own struct and then park a pointer to it on the kernel’s side so that every later callback can get it back. The chain is short — what makes it worth a figure is that it is walked from memory every single time.probe 里的四行，画出来。内核给你一个 struct device；你分配自己的结构体，然后把指向它的指针寄存在内核那边，让之后每一次回调都能取回来。链很短 —— 值得画成图，是因为它每次都是凭记忆走的。

5 · Bitfields, and what __packed does to them5 · 位域，以及 __packed 对它做了什么

Two things the source does not show. First, bitfields are cut out of a storage unit the compiler picks, filling from the least significant bit on a little-endian target — so mode is bits 2:0, not the top of the byte. Second, __packed does not just make the struct smaller: it moves every later field, and both of the ones after the bitfield land off their natural boundary — the u16 at offset 1, the u32 at offset 3. On arm64 that read is fixed up in a trap handler; on some 32-bit cores it is a fault. The figure puts both views under the source: the bit strip for the unit, the byte list for what moved.源码上看不出来的两件事。第一，位域是从编译器挑的一个存储单元里切出来的，小端目标上从最低位开始填 —— 所以 mode 是 bit 2:0，不是那个字节的高位。第二，__packed 不只是把结构体变小：它会把后面每个字段都往前挪，位域后面那两个字段都落在了非自然边界上 —— u16 在偏移 1，u32 在偏移 3。arm64 上这次读会掉进处理非对齐访问的陷阱里，有些 32 位核上直接是异常。这张图把两个视角都摆在源码下面：位带看单元怎么切，字节表看什么挪了位置。

6 · A union is not a struct with fewer bytes6 · 联合体不是「字段少一点的结构体」

An offset list cannot draw a union, because an offset list assumes one field comes after another. Here every member starts at byte 0 and they sit on top of each other. That is the whole point and it is also the whole danger: writing one member and reading another does not convert anything, it re-reads the same bytes under a different type. The bar that is longest sets the size; the tail nobody covers is still allocated.偏移表画不出联合体，因为偏移表的前提是「一个字段接着一个字段」。这里每个成员都从第 0 字节开始，互相压在一起。这既是它的全部用处，也是它的全部风险：写了一个成员再读另一个，不会做任何转换，只是换个类型把同一批字节再读一遍。最长的那根决定大小；没人盖到的尾巴照样占着内存。

The bug this figure is about: user space fills point (4 bytes) and the driver reads stamp (8 bytes). Bytes 4–7 were never written, so the driver reads whatever was on the stack — and it reads it successfully, every time, with a different value. Copy the union in full or check which member the ioctl number promised; the type system will not do it for you.这张图讲的那个 bug：用户态填的是 point（4 字节），驱动读的是 stamp（8 字节）。第 4–7 字节从来没被写过，驱动读到的是栈上残留的东西 —— 而且每次都读成功，每次值还不一样。要么整个联合体拷全，要么按 ioctl 号确认该读哪个成员；类型系统不会替你挡。
